Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ List

How do I register on StudentHub?

Click the Register link in the navigation. Fill in your personal, contact, and academic details, set a password, and submit the form. Once approved by the admin, you'll receive a confirmation email.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Visit the Login page and click Forgot your password?. Enter your registered email address, and a password-reset link will be sent to you within 5 minutes.

When are semester results published?

Results are published on the portal within 30 days after the examination ends. You'll receive an email notification when your results are available on the Dashboard.

How do I update my personal information?

Log in and navigate to My Profile. You can update your mobile number and address directly. For changes to your name or date of birth, contact the admin office.

How do I register for an event?

Go to the Events page, find the event you're interested in, and click Register Now. You must be logged in to register. Confirmation is sent to your registered email.

Is my personal data secure on this portal?

Yes. StudentHub uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stores passwords in hashed form. We do not share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Who do I contact for technical issues?

Use the Contact Us form or email support@studenthub.edu. Our team responds within 1 business day.

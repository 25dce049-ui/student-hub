About StudentHub

Our Mission

StudentHub aims to streamline every aspect of student life — from course management and result tracking to event discovery and peer collaboration — through an intuitive, accessible web portal.

Our Vision

We envision a campus where technology removes barriers so that every student, regardless of ability or device, can access the information and resources they need to succeed.

Core Values

♿ Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA-compliant pages so every student can navigate comfortably.

🔒 Privacy

Student data is handled securely; we never share personal information.

🌐 Openness

Open standards and transparent processes guide every feature we build.

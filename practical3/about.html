About StudentHub

Empowering students with technology, accessibility, and community.

Our Mission

StudentHub aims to streamline every aspect of student life through an intuitive, accessible, and responsive web portal — making academic information available anytime, anywhere, on any device.

Core Values

  • 2022StudentHub v1.0 launched – Basic course & result portal.
  • 2023Events module added – 500+ students signed up in the first week.
  • 2024Mobile-responsive redesign – Bootstrap 5 + custom CSS.
  • 2025Accessibility overhaul – WCAG 2.1 AA certification.
  • 20261,200+ active students – Multi-campus rollout underway.
